Moving checklist: an 8-week timeline

The easiest way to move without chaos is to work backward from moving day. Book help and sort belongings 8 weeks out, handle address changes and utilities 4 weeks out, pack room by room in the last two weeks, and keep a separate first-night box. Copy the timeline below and adjust the dates.

How to use this moving checklist

Write your moving date at the top, then count back and put a date next to each heading. If you have less than 8 weeks, start at the heading that matches the time you have and do the earlier sections first, quickly. Most people underestimate packing and overestimate how much they will want to unpack, so decluttering early pays off twice: fewer boxes to pack, and fewer boxes to open later.

  1. Copy the full timeline.
  2. Delete what does not apply (for example, school records if you have no kids).
  3. Each week, pull that week’s tasks into a short list you look at every day.
  4. Keep one running list of “people and services to notify” — it always grows.

8 to 6 weeks before

  • ☐ Set a moving budget and decide: professional movers, rental truck, or friends
  • ☐ Request quotes from movers and read what each quote includes (packing, stairs, insurance)
  • ☐ Book your mover or truck as soon as you choose — month-end and summer weekends fill up
  • ☐ Walk through every room and sort into keep, sell, donate, recycle
  • ☐ Start using up frozen food and pantry items
  • ☐ Create a moving folder for leases, quotes, receipts and inventory
  • ☐ If you rent, read your lease for notice periods and move-out cleaning rules
  • ☐ Measure large furniture and the doorways at the new place

4 weeks before

  • ☐ Gather boxes, tape, markers, bubble wrap and a few wardrobe boxes
  • ☐ Submit a change of address with your postal service
  • ☐ Update address with your bank, employer, insurance, subscriptions and online stores
  • ☐ Schedule utility shut-off at the old place and start-up at the new one (power, water, gas, internet)
  • ☐ Transfer school or medical records if needed
  • ☐ Book time off work for moving day and the day after
  • ☐ Start packing things you rarely use: books, off-season clothes, decor

2 weeks before

  • ☐ Pack room by room, labeling each box with room and a short content list
  • ☐ Confirm date, time and address with movers or helpers
  • ☐ Arrange parking or elevator booking at both places if needed
  • ☐ Return borrowed items and pick up dry cleaning or repairs
  • ☐ Refill prescriptions so you are not hunting for a pharmacy in week one
  • ☐ Plan meals that use up what is left

The week before

  • ☐ Pack the first-night box: phone chargers, toiletries, towels, sheets, medicine, snacks, toilet paper, basic tools
  • ☐ Defrost and clean the fridge if it is moving
  • ☐ Take photos of the old place’s condition and meter readings
  • ☐ Set aside documents, keys, valuables and laptop to carry yourself
  • ☐ Confirm payment method for movers

Moving day

  • ☐ Final walk-through of every room, closet, cupboard and drawer
  • ☐ Check that the truck inventory matches your list before it leaves
  • ☐ Lock windows, turn off lights, hand over or leave keys as agreed
  • ☐ At the new place: check for damage and note meter readings
  • ☐ Direct boxes to the right rooms; open the first-night box first
  • ☐ Make the beds before you do anything else

First week after the move

  • ☐ Unpack kitchen and bathroom first, then bedrooms
  • ☐ Test smoke detectors and find the breaker panel and main water valve
  • ☐ Change or rekey locks if possible
  • ☐ Update the address on your driver’s license, vehicle registration and voter registration as your local rules require
  • ☐ Register with a local doctor or pharmacy if you moved far
  • ☐ Break down boxes and recycle packing material

Frequently asked questions

When should I start my moving checklist?

About 8 weeks ahead is comfortable. With less time, compress the timeline but keep the order: book help, declutter, change addresses, then pack.

What goes in a first-night box?

Chargers, toiletries, towels, sheets, medicine, snacks, toilet paper, a few dishes and basic tools. Carry it with you, not in the truck.

Who do I need to notify when I move?

Your postal service, bank, employer, insurance, utilities, internet provider, subscriptions, doctors, schools and any government agencies your local rules require.
