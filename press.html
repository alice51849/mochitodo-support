Mochi: Cute Checklist
Press & media

Mochi is a cute, cozy to-do list and checklist: write down what you need to do, then tap the little circle when it is done.

Key features

  • Simple checklists for school, work and groceries
  • Tap the circle to check off an item
  • Reminders on the tasks that matter
  • Switchable cute themes for your list
  • Free with 2 lists of up to 4 items each; an in-app purchase unlocks unlimited lists and items
