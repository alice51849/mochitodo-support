How to plan for midterms and finals

  1. Collect every date. Midterms, finals, assignment due dates and labs, from your course outlines and your school’s exam schedule.
  2. Split each course into topics. Use the course outline or syllabus; one topic per study block.
  3. Work backwards. The first exam gets topics first; keep the last days before each exam for practice questions and old exams if your instructor shares them.
  4. Fit study around your week. Classes, shifts and the commute go in first; study blocks go in the gaps. A bus or train ride is good for flashcards.
  5. Keep one evening free. Rest makes the schedule last through exam season.

Four tasks, done, then stop. Consistency beats one all-nighter.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I start studying for finals?

Start your plan as soon as the exam schedule is out. Even two short blocks a day for a few weeks covers more than cramming at the end.

How do I study with a part-time job?

Put shifts and classes in first, then study blocks in the gaps, and use the commute for flashcards so busy days still count.
