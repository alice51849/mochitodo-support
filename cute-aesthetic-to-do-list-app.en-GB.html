What makes a to-do list app &ldquo;cute&rdquo; and still useful

People searching for a cute or aesthetic to-do list are rarely asking for decoration only. They have usually tried a plain task app, found it looked like a spreadsheet, and stopped opening it. A cute list works when the look gives you a reason to come back, and the basics still do their job:

  • It stays in sight. A Home Screen widget shows today’s tasks every time you unlock your phone.
  • Finishing feels like something. In Mochi you tap the little circle when a task is done, which the store listing calls the gentle satisfaction of bringing order to your day.
  • It matches your phone. Pick a theme whose colours suit your wallpaper, so the widget looks like it belongs there.
  • You can scan it at a glance. An emoji per task (a book for reading, a basket for the shop) is quicker to read than a column of text.
  • It reminds you when it matters. Reminders are there for things with a time.

A cute daily list you can copy

Keep today to four tasks (the free limit per list, and a realistic number for a school or work day). Put everything else in a second list or a note.

Today

  • ☐ 📚 Read 10 pages or one chapter
  • ☐ 💻 Finish one piece of work or homework
  • ☐ 📞 Reply to the message you keep putting off
  • ☐ 🧢 Tidy your desk for ten minutes

Errands

  • ☐ 🛒 Milk, bread, fruit
  • ☐ 💊 Pick up prescription
  • ☐ 📦 Return the parcel
  • ☐ 🎁 Card for a birthday this week

Turn on a reminder only for the tasks that have a time. Leave the rest without alerts, so the alerts still mean something.

Five-minute setup on iPhone

  1. Install Mochi and create a list called Today.
  2. Choose one of the five free themes that suits your wallpaper colours.
  3. Add your four tasks and give each one an emoji you recognise at a glance.
  4. Switch on a reminder for anything with a time, such as a deadline or a call.
  5. Add the Mochi widget: touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap the edit or + button, search for Mochi and pick a size. The widget guide has details.
  6. Use the second list for errands, and tick items off on Apple Watch while you are out.

Step-by-step help for tasks and reminders is in the Mochi checklist guide.

Honest note about the free version: Mochi’s free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set. That is enough for a short “Today” list and one more list, but not for every item on this page. Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets. If you stay on the free version, keep the full plan in a note or on paper and put only the four tasks that matter most today in Mochi, so the list stays short and organised.
