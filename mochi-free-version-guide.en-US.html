How to use Mochi’s free version: a two-list, four-task daily routine

Mochi’s free version gives you 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set (from the US App Store listing). That sounds small, but a short list is easier to finish. This guide shows a simple routine that fits inside those limits: one list for today, one for the things you must not forget, and a two-minute reset each evening.

Step 1: Set up your two lists

With two lists, give each one a clear job:

  • Today: only what has to happen today.
  • Don’t forget: groceries, an errand, something to bring tomorrow.

Everything else (ideas, someday plans, long projects) stays out of the app for now. Keeping the lists short is what makes them work.

Step 2: Choose today’s four

Each free list holds up to 4 tasks, so the Today list asks you to choose. A quick way to pick:

  1. One thing with a deadline (“Return library books”).
  2. One thing for your home or yourself (“Laundry”, “20-minute walk”).
  3. One thing for school or work (“Finish slide 3”).
  4. One small win you can do in five minutes (“Water the plant”).

If a task is too big to finish today, write the next small step instead: “Outline essay” rather than “Write essay”.

Step 3: Emoji, reminders and the circle

  • Emoji: give each task its own little emoji so you can read your list at a glance (🧺 laundry, 📚 library, 🌱 plant). The emoji on this page are only examples; pick from the emoji set in your app.
  • Reminders: set a reminder for anything tied to a time, like “Call the dentist at 10”.
  • The circle: tap the circle when a task is done. Four taps and your day’s list is complete.

You can also pick one of the 5 free themes so the list looks the way you like.

Step 4: The two-minute evening reset

  1. Look at what you checked off on Today.
  2. If something is still unfinished, decide whether it still matters. If it does, make it one of tomorrow’s four.
  3. Check Don’t forget: remove what you bought or did, add what you need for tomorrow.
  4. Choose tomorrow’s four.

When two lists are not enough

If you find yourself wanting a third list (say, one per class or a trip) or more than four tasks on a list, that is what Mochi Premium is for. Premium is an in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets. For a side-by-side look and a one-week test to decide, see Mochi free vs Premium.
