Chore list app: a chore checklist you actually see

Chore lists fail when they live on the fridge door or deep inside an app. Keep two short ones instead, daily and weekly, and put them where you look all day: your iPhone Home Screen. Below are two chore checklists for a Canadian household you can copy, and how to set them up in Mochi. Feature details are taken from Mochi’s App Store listing.

Split chores into daily and weekly

  • Daily chores are the small things that pile up if you skip a day: dishes, a quick tidy, the lunch bags.
  • Weekly chores are the bigger jobs: vacuuming, laundry, bathrooms and getting the bins out on collection day.
  • Four items each keeps both lists finishable, and fits Mochi’s free version (2 lists with up to 4 tasks each).

Chore checklists to copy

Daily chores

  • ☐ 🍽️ Dishwasher loaded or emptied
  • ☐ 🧽 Wipe the kitchen counters
  • ☐ 👢 Boots and coats back on the mat and hooks
  • ☐ 🥪 Lunches packed for tomorrow

Weekly chores

  • ☐ 🗑️ Garbage, recycling and compost out on collection day
  • ☐ 🧺 Laundry washed, dried and put away
  • ☐ 🧹 Vacuum and mop the main floor
  • ☐ 🚿 Clean the bathroom

In winter, swap one weekly chore for ❄️ Clear the walkway and steps after a snowfall and set a reminder for it.

Set it up on iPhone

  1. Install Mochi and create two lists: Daily chores and Weekly chores.
  2. Add four chores to each and give every chore its own emoji so the list reads at a glance.
  3. Set a reminder for anything tied to a day, such as collection day.
  4. Add the Mochi widget: touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Mochi. (The App Store listing does not say which plan the widget needs, so check in the app.)
  5. Tap the circle when a chore is done.

Need more lists or more than four chores per list? Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest way to keep a chore list?

Keep two short lists, daily and weekly, with about four chores each, and keep them where you will see them. Mochi lets you keep your list on a Home Screen widget and Apple Watch.

Can I see my chore checklist without opening the app?

Yes. Mochi has a Home Screen widget. The App Store listing does not say which plan the widget needs, so check in the app.

Can Mochi remind me about collection day?

Yes. Mochi includes helpful reminders for busy days.

How many lists can I make for free?

The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set. Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.
