Halloween Party Checklist 2026: A To-Do List You Can Copy

Short answer: plan a Halloween party in three passes. Two weeks out, lock the guest list, theme and costumes. The week of, shop and prep decorations. On the day, set up, cook and do a quick safety walk. Halloween 2026 falls on Saturday, October 31, so many parties will be that evening or the night before. Below is the full checklist with checkboxes you can copy into a note or a to-do app, followed by a short “top 4” version that fits on one small list and a few steps for putting it into Mochi: Cute Checklist on your iPhone.

Two weeks before (around October 17)

The early decisions are the ones that are hard to change later. Get them done first so the rest of the list is just shopping and setup.

  • ☐ Pick the date and time (Saturday, Oct 31, or Friday, Oct 30, if guests go trick-or-treating on the 31st)
  • ☐ Decide indoor, backyard or both, and a rain plan
  • ☐ Write the guest list and send invitations with a reply-by date
  • ☐ Choose a theme (classic spooky, movie night, kid-friendly costume party)
  • ☐ Order or plan costumes for everyone in your household
  • ☐ Ask guests about food allergies, especially for kids' parties
  • ☐ Plan 2–3 activities: costume parade, pumpkin decorating, a scary-movie corner
  • ☐ Check what decorations you already own before buying more

The week of the party

This is the busiest week. Splitting it into shopping, food and decorations keeps it from turning into one long list you avoid looking at.

Shopping

  • ☐ Candy and treats, including a few allergy-friendly options
  • ☐ Party food and drinks (plan finger foods that are easy to eat in costume)
  • ☐ Plates, cups, napkins and trash bags
  • ☐ Pumpkins for carving or decorating
  • ☐ Batteries for flameless candles and string lights
  • ☐ Prizes or small favors for games

Prep

  • ☐ Carve or decorate pumpkins (two or three days ahead is plenty)
  • ☐ Make a short music playlist
  • ☐ Prepare any make-ahead food and freeze or refrigerate it
  • ☐ Try on costumes and fix anything that rips, trips or blocks vision
  • ☐ Send a reminder message to guests with the address and start time

On the day: setup and a quick safety walk

  • ☐ Put decorations up, keeping walkways and exits clear
  • ☐ Use flameless candles in pumpkins and near costumes
  • ☐ Light the porch and path to the door
  • ☐ Set out food with labels for common allergens
  • ☐ Put candy for trick-or-treaters in a bowl by the door
  • ☐ Charge your phone and set a reminder for the start time
  • ☐ Make a spot for coats, bags and costume accessories
  • ☐ Plan who cleans up what after the party

If kids are heading out to trick-or-treat, agree on a route, a return time and a check-in plan before the party starts. Costumes that are easy to see in the dark and masks that do not block vision help.

The short version: your Halloween &ldquo;top 4&rdquo;

A long list is useful for planning, but on a busy week you only need to see what matters now. Here is a four-item version for each stage that fits on one small list.

List “Halloween this week”

  1. Send invitations and reminders
  2. Costumes ready and tried on
  3. Candy, food and drinks bought
  4. Pumpkins and decorations done

List “Party day”

  1. Decorations up, walkways clear
  2. Flameless candles and porch light on
  3. Food out with allergy labels
  4. Candy bowl by the door

Frequently asked questions

When is Halloween in 2026?

Halloween is on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Many families hold parties that evening, or on Friday, October 30, if they plan to go trick-or-treating on the 31st.

How far ahead should I plan a Halloween party?

About two weeks is comfortable for a home party: invitations and costumes first, then shopping and decorations the week of, and setup on the day.

What should a Halloween party checklist include?

Guest list and invitations, costumes, food and drinks, candy for trick-or-treaters, decorations, activities, a safety check (clear walkways, flameless candles, porch light) and a cleanup plan.
