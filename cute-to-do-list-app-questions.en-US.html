Frequently asked questions

What makes Mochi different from a plain to-do list?

Every task can get its own little emoji, you can switch between 101 themes, and checking things off is designed to feel calm and satisfying instead of like another chore.

Can I see my list without opening the app?

Yes. Mochi has a Home Screen widget and an Apple Watch app, so your list is always a glance away.

What can I use it for?

School, work, errands, groceries, habits and daily routines, all in one place. It works as a simple checklist, a daily planner and a habit tracker.

Does it remind me about tasks?

Yes. Mochi includes reminders that keep you on track.

What’s included for free?

The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set.

What does Premium add?

Premium is an in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.
