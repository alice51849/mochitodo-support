Why a widget helps with chores

Chores rarely get skipped because they are hard. They get skipped because nobody sees the list. A widget fixes that: every time you unlock your phone, today’s few chores are right there.

  • Home Screen widget and Apple Watch: your list is a glance away, at home or out on errands.
  • An emoji for every task: 🧺 laundry, 🍽️ dishes, 🪴 plants. You recognize each chore before you read it.
  • Tap the circle when it’s done: a tiny moment of calm instead of a wall of unchecked boxes.
  • Reminders: set one for the chore with a deadline, like trash night.
  • 101 themes (5 free, 96 with Premium) so your list matches your wallpaper.

Set it up in five minutes

  1. Install Mochi and make a list called Chores, and a second one called Errands.
  2. Add up to four tasks to each and give every task an emoji.
  3. Pick one of the 5 free themes that suit your wallpaper.
  4. Turn on a reminder for anything with a time.
  5. Add the Mochi widget: touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Mochi. (The App Store listing does not say which plan the widget needs, so check in the app.)

Frequently asked questions

Does Mochi have a to-do list widget?

Yes. Mochi has a Home Screen widget, so your list stays in sight, and an Apple Watch app for checking it from your wrist.

Can I use Mochi as a chores list?

Yes. The App Store listing describes a simple checklist for tasks and errands with reminders, plus habit and routine tracking, so household chores fit in a list of their own.

What makes it feel cozy?

Every task can get its own little emoji, you tap a circle when it is done, and you can pick from 101 themes (5 free, 96 with Premium).

What can I do with the free version?

The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set.

What does Premium add?

Premium is an in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.
