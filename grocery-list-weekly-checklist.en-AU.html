Grocery list and weekly checklist: templates for Australia

Short answer: a weekly grocery list works well when you plan meals first, check the fridge and cupboards, and sort the list by aisle, so one shop at Woolies, Coles or Aldi covers the week. Pair it with a short weekly household checklist and the week runs itself. How to write a weekly grocery list in 10 minutes

  1. Plan four or five dinners. Leave the other nights for leftovers or eating out.
  2. Check what you have. Fridge, freezer and cupboards before you write anything.
  3. Write by aisle. Fruit and veg, then the deli and fridge, then the pantry aisles, then household. Sorting the list this way saves walking back.
  4. Add the staples you are running low on. Keep a running list during the week and copy it in.
  5. Tick off as you shop. On a watch or phone, so the list is in your hand, not on the fridge door at home.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a weekly grocery list?

Plan four or five dinners, check the fridge and cupboards, then write what is missing sorted by aisle, plus any staples that are running low.

Can I use Mochi as a shopping list?

Yes. The App Store listing names shopping and grocery lists alongside tasks, routines and notes.

Can I tick off my shopping on Apple Watch?

Yes. The listing says you can check your list from Apple Watch.

Is Mochi free?

Yes. The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set. Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.

Is Mochi connected to any supermarket?

No. Supermarket names on this page are only examples of where people shop in Australia; Mochi is a checklist app and is not linked to any store.

Should I do one big shop or several small ones?

Many people do one planned big shop plus a quick top-up for fresh food midweek. A written list keeps both short.
