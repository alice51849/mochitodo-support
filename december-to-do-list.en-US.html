December To-Do List 2026: A Week-by-Week Holiday Checklist

Short answer: split December into four short lists instead of one long one. Week 1 is for planning (budget, gift list, calendar). Week 2 is for buying and shipping. Week 3 is for cards, food and travel. Christmas week (December 25, 2026 is a Friday) is for wrapping, cooking and resting, and the last days of the month are for wrapping up the year. Below is a December checklist with boxes you can copy, a “top 4” for each week, and how to keep it in Mochi: Cute Checklist on your iPhone.

Week 1 (December 1–6): plan the month

  • ☐ Write every December date you already know on one calendar: parties, school events, travel, work deadlines
  • ☐ Set a gift budget and write the gift list, one line per person
  • ☐ Decide what you are hosting and what you are attending
  • ☐ Check whether anything needs to be ordered or booked early (travel, a venue, a large food order)
  • ☐ Pick one evening a week as your “holiday admin” time

Week 2 (December 7–13): buy and ship

  • ☐ Buy or order gifts that have to be shipped
  • ☐ Look up your carrier's posted holiday shipping deadlines and set a reminder a few days before the one you need
  • ☐ Write the card list and buy cards and stamps
  • ☐ Book anything for Christmas week that fills up: haircuts, pet sitter, car service
  • ☐ Start a running grocery list for holiday meals

Week 3 (December 14–20): cards, food and travel

  • ☐ Write and send cards
  • ☐ Ship the last packages before your carrier's deadline
  • ☐ Plan holiday meals and split the grocery list into “keeps” and “buy fresh”
  • ☐ If you are traveling, confirm tickets and start a packing list
  • ☐ Buy wrapping paper, tape and gift tags

Christmas week (December 21–27)

  • ☐ Wrap gifts in one sitting and label them
  • ☐ Buy the “buy fresh” groceries
  • ☐ Christmas Eve (Thursday, December 24): set out what you need for the next morning
  • ☐ Christmas Day (Friday, December 25): keep the list short and enjoy it
  • ☐ After the day: write down who sent what, so thank-you notes are easy

The last days of December (December 28–31)

New Year's Eve is Thursday, December 31, 2026. Keep this week for wrapping up the year: returns, thank-you notes and a quick look back. For the full wrap-up list, see the year-end checklist.

  • ☐ Make any gift returns or exchanges
  • ☐ Send thank-you notes
  • ☐ Take down and store decorations, or schedule a day in January to do it
  • ☐ Write three things that went well this year

How to keep it in Mochi

  1. Install Mochi: Cute Checklist and create a list called “December.”
  2. Add the four tasks for the week you are in, and give each one its own emoji so the list is easy to scan.
  3. Turn on reminders for the dated steps: the shipping deadline you looked up, the card mailing day and the grocery run.
  4. Add a Mochi widget to your Home Screen (see the to-do list widget guide), and keep the list on Apple Watch while you are out shopping.
  5. Use your second list as the gift or grocery list, then tap the little circle as each item is done.

Frequently asked questions

What should be on a December to-do list?

Plan the month (calendar, gift budget, gift list), buy and ship gifts early, send cards, plan holiday meals and travel, then use the last days of December to wrap up the year with returns and thank-you notes.

What day is Christmas in 2026?

Christmas Day 2026 is a Friday, December 25. Christmas Eve is Thursday, December 24, and New Year's Eve is Thursday, December 31.

When should I ship holiday gifts?

Earlier than you think. Look up your carrier's posted holiday shipping deadlines for the service you plan to use, then set a reminder a few days before that date.

Does Mochi have a Christmas or holiday mode?

No. Mochi is a general cute to-do list. You can make your own “December” list, pick emoji for each task, turn on reminders and keep it on a Home Screen widget.

Will the free version of Mochi hold a December checklist?

The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, which fits one week's top four plus a short gift or grocery list. Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks.
