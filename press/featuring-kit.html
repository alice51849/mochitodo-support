The story

A cute to-do checklist: tap to check off, plus widgets. Mochi is the cute, cozy to-do list where every task can get its own little emoji. You tap the circle when you’re done and enjoy that tiny burst of calm. School, work, errands, groceries, habits and daily routines live in one adorable place, and the list stays close at hand on a Home Screen widget and on Apple Watch. With 101 themes and 11 emoji sets to choose from (5 themes and 1 emoji set are free), the list can look the way you like, which makes it nicer to open again tomorrow. Mochi is made for people who want a softer, prettier and more motivated everyday routine — a small, friendly start rather than a complicated system.

  • Optimized for iPhone Duo: the app fills the entire inner display and automatically adjusts its layout whether the device is folded or open

How the free version and Premium work

The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set. Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.

For current pricing, please see the App Store product page.

Why it fits getting back to routine in January

The first weeks of January are when the holidays end and everyday life starts up again: school drop-offs, work, groceries, the errands that piled up over the break. Mochi’s free version is enough for a simple restart — two lists of up to four tasks each, for example one for daily routines and one for the week’s errands — and the cute themes and emoji make the list something you want to come back to, one tap at a time.
