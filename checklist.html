How to use Mochi, the cute checklist app for iPhone

Mochi is a to-do list and checklist app for iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later) with an Apple Watch app. This guide covers everyday use: adding tasks, ticking them off, reminders, lists, themes and what the free version includes. It describes the app as it works today.

Add and tick off tasks

  1. Open Mochi. On first launch a small sample list is already there so you can see how it works.
  2. Tap the Add a to-do… row at the end of the list, type your task, then tap Done on the keyboard (or tap Add).
  3. Tap the circle next to a task when it is finished. Tap it again to undo. The badge above the list shows how many tasks are left.

The ⋯ menu next to that badge holds the list tools. Clear completed removes the ticked tasks, Uncheck all resets the whole list (handy for a daily routine), and Completed To-dos shows the tasks you have ticked off and not cleared yet.

Edit, move and delete tasks

  • Swipe a task to the right to edit it, or to the left to delete it.
  • Touch and hold a task for more choices: Edit, Duplicate, Move up, Move down and Delete.
  • To drag tasks into a new order, open the ⋯ menu, choose Reorder, drag, then tap Done.

The edit screen lets you change the title, pick an emoji, add a note and set a reminder.

Reminders and repeats

  1. Open a task's edit screen and switch on Remind me. The first time, iOS asks whether Mochi may send notifications; tap Allow.
  2. Pick the date and time.
  3. Under Repeat, choose once, daily, weekly or monthly. Weekly repeats on the same weekday and monthly on the same day of the month, at the time you picked.

Ticking a task pauses its reminder. Unticking it (or using Uncheck all) turns a repeating reminder back on; a one-time reminder comes back only if its time is still in the future. Reminders are scheduled on your device, so they need no account and no internet connection. If nothing arrives, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Mochi To-Do.

Keep separate lists

You can keep separate lists, such as Today, Work or Groceries. They appear as small tabs at the top of the screen (a side column on larger screens). Tap + to create a list with its own name and emoji, tap a tab to switch lists, and touch and hold a tab to rename or delete it.

Change the look with themes

Tap the paint palette icon at the top of the screen to open Skins, the app's name for themes. Tap one to apply it to the current list, or touch and hold to preview it first. Each list can have its own theme. Five themes are free; locked ones show a lock icon and need Premium.

Widgets and Apple Watch

To see and tick off your checklist from the Home Screen or Lock Screen without opening the app, follow the widget setup guide. The Apple Watch app shows your checklist, lets you tick tasks with a tap and add a new task by dictation.

What the free version includes

Mochi is free to download. The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set. Premium is an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets; it is not a subscription. If you reinstall the app, open Settings inside it and tap Restore Purchase.

Your data stays on your device

Your lists are stored on your device: there is no account, no ads and no tracking. That also means there is no cloud sync between devices; the Apple Watch app receives your list from the paired iPhone.

Troubleshooting

I can't add another task

The free version holds up to 4 tasks per list, and ticked tasks count too. Use Clear completed to make room, create the second free list, or unlock Premium for unlimited tasks.

My reminder didn't go off

Check that Remind me is on, that a one-time reminder is still in the future, that the task is not ticked, and that notifications for Mochi To-Do are allowed in iPhone Settings.

How do I change the app's language?

Tap the gear icon at the top of the screen, then Language. The first option, System, follows your iPhone's language.
