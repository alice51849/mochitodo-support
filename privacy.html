Privacy Policy

Mochi To-Do · Last updated: September 18, 2026

Mochi To-Do does not collect any personal data. The app is built to be private by design. You don't need an account, and you never sign in.

What we store

  • Your lists, tasks, skins and settings are stored only on your device. On iPhone and iPad, they also live in your private iCloud-backed app group so your widgets stay in sync. On Android, there are no widgets or companion watch app, and your data never leaves your device — it is not backed up to any cloud service.
  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your tasks or any personal information.

Analytics & tracking

  • No analytics SDKs. No advertising. No third-party trackers.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

  • If you set a reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device. The reminder content never leaves your device.

Purchases

  • The optional one-time unlock is processed by the store you installed the app from — Apple via the App Store on iPhone and iPad, or Google Play Billing on Android. The store handles payment; we never receive your payment details. Purchase status is checked on your device only.

Children

  • Mochi To-Do does not collect data from anyone, including children.
