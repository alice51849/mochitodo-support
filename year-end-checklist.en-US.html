Year-End Checklist 2026: Wrap Up the Year Before January

Short answer: a year-end checklist is about closing things, not setting goals. In the last weeks of December, finish the paperwork, clear out the house and your phone, close open loops at work and take ten minutes to look back. Goals can wait for January. Below is a year-end checklist with boxes you can copy, a “top 4” version and how to keep it in Mochi: Cute Checklist on your iPhone.

Money and paperwork

  • ☐ Gather this year's receipts and statements in one folder
  • ☐ Check whether any account, benefit or plan you use has a December 31 deadline, and confirm it with the provider or a tax professional
  • ☐ Review recurring charges and cancel the ones you no longer use
  • ☐ Write down what you gave to charity this year and keep the receipts
  • ☐ Note important dates for next year: renewals, appointments, birthdays

Home

  • ☐ Clear out the refrigerator and pantry before holiday cooking
  • ☐ Donate clothes and items you did not use this year
  • ☐ Check smoke and carbon monoxide alarms
  • ☐ Replace burned-out bulbs and empty batteries
  • ☐ Put a “put away decorations” day on the calendar for January

Digital clean-up

  • ☐ Back up your phone and photos
  • ☐ Delete old screenshots and duplicate photos
  • ☐ Unsubscribe from emails you never open
  • ☐ Update apps and your phone's software
  • ☐ Clear out your to-do list: finish, move or delete every old task

Work and school

  • ☐ Finish or hand off anything due before the break
  • ☐ Set an out-of-office message for the days you are away
  • ☐ Write a short list of where you left each project, so January-you can start quickly
  • ☐ Clean your desk and your downloads folder

A short look back

  • ☐ Write three things that went well this year
  • ☐ Write one thing you want to do differently
  • ☐ Send one thank-you message to someone who helped you

When this list is done, move on to the New Year goals checklist for 2027.

The short version: a “top 4”

  1. Receipts and statements in one folder
  2. Phone and photos backed up
  3. Unused subscriptions cancelled
  4. Three good things from this year written down

Have more time? Make a second “top 4” from the home and work sections.

Frequently asked questions

What should be on a year-end checklist?

Money and paperwork (receipts, deadlines, subscriptions), home (clear out, donate, safety checks), a digital clean-up (backups, photos, email), work hand-offs and a short look back at the year.

How is a year-end checklist different from New Year resolutions?

A year-end checklist closes things from the year that is ending. New Year goals are about what you start next. Doing the year-end list first makes the January list shorter.

When should I start my year-end checklist?

Early to mid December, so anything with a December 31 deadline is not left to the last day. New Year's Eve 2026 is Thursday, December 31.

Does Mochi have a special year-end feature?

No. You use Mochi's ordinary lists: add tasks, give each one an emoji, turn on reminders and keep the list on a Home Screen widget or Apple Watch.
