Kawaii to-do list app: how Mochi works on iPhone

Short answer: Mochi is a cute, cozy to-do list for iPhone and Apple Watch. Every task can get its own little emoji, and you tap the circle when you're done. You can switch between 101 themes and keep the list on a Home Screen widget, so it is the first thing you see when you pick up your phone. Below: what the App Store listing says it does, a two-list setup to start with, and who should pick something else.

What Mochi does, according to its App Store listing

  • A simple checklist for tasks and errands, where each task can have its own little emoji.
  • Reminders that keep you on track.
  • A daily planner, agenda and schedule.
  • Habit and routine tracking.
  • Notes, grocery lists, widgets and themes: 101 themes, 5 to start with and 96 with Premium.
  • Apple Watch, so you can check things off without taking out your phone.
  • iPhone Duo: the app fills the entire inner display, folded or open.

The kawaii part is not decoration only. A list you like looking at is a list you open again tomorrow, and the widget puts it where you already look.

A two-list setup to start with

From the start you get 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set. Four tasks a list is enough if you keep each list short and finishable:

Today

  • ☐ 📚 Read chapter 4
  • ☐ 🧺 Start a load of laundry
  • ☐ 💌 Reply to Mom
  • ☐ 🥤 Drink water after lunch

Errands

  • ☐ 🛒 Milk and eggs
  • ☐ 📦 Return the package
  • ☐ 💊 Pick up vitamins
  • ☐ 🌷 Water the plants

When a list is done, clear it and write the next four. Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.

Make it a list you keep opening

  1. Add the widget first. The list on your Home Screen reminds you on its own.
  2. Give every task an emoji. It makes the list readable at a glance.
  3. Set a reminder only on the things that have a time.
  4. Pick a theme you like. Change it when the list starts to feel stale.

Who Mochi is not for

The App Store listing does not mention shared lists, team projects or boards. Mochi is a personal to-do list for one person's everyday life. If you need to assign tasks to coworkers or family members, look for an app that describes shared lists.

Want to do this on your iPhone? Download Mochi, add the widget, write four tasks with an emoji each, and tap the circle when you're done.
