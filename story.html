About Mochi: who makes it, how it is made and what it promises

Mochi is a to-do list built to feel good to use: tap a small circle and the task is checked off. This page explains who is behind it, how it is built and exactly where AI is involved.

What Mochi is

Mochi is a cute checklist for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. You add tasks, tap the circle when you finish, set reminders, split things into lists and give each list its own theme. It needs iOS 17 or later. Step-by-step instructions are in the usage guide.

Who makes it

Mochi is made and maintained by the small independent team behind Lumi Studio, which publishes a set of iPhone apps (you can see the others at the bottom of the support home page). The developer name shown on the App Store listing is Guan Feng Lai. It is a small independent project, and support email goes to the team directly.

What we promise

  • Free to start. The free version has clear limits: 2 lists, up to 4 tasks per list, 5 themes and 1 emoji set.
  • One optional, one-time purchase unlocks the rest. There is no subscription.
  • No account, no ads and no tracking. Your lists stay on your device.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device, so they work without an internet connection.

How the app looks

The 101 in-app themes are generated by our own code from gradients, paper textures and simple shapes. The app icon is an AI-generated illustration. The App Store screenshots show the real app screens with a short caption added.

Where AI is involved

The text on this site, including these guides, is drafted with AI assistance and checked against the app before it goes live. The app's translations into many languages were produced with AI translation tools. If a phrase sounds wrong in your language, please tell us and we will fix it. The app itself has no AI features and no chatbot.

Contact and feedback

Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome at hourstag.app@gmail.com. We typically reply within 1–2 business days.
