To do list or checklist: what is the difference?

People search for both, and in practice they overlap:

  • A to do list is what you plan to get done: today, this week, for a class or a job. It changes every day.
  • A checklist is a fixed set of steps you repeat: a morning routine, a packing list, a closing shift. You reuse it.

An app that handles both needs four things: quick capture, a list that stays in sight, a satisfying way to tick things off and alerts for anything with a time. Mochi’s App Store listing covers each: a fast checklist for tasks and errands, helpful reminders for busy days, adorable Home Screen widgets and checking tasks from Apple Watch.

A ten-minute daily method

  1. Brain dump once. Write everything on your mind into one note.
  2. Pick today’s four. Move four tasks into a list called Today.
  3. Time only what has a time. A shift, a class deadline or an appointment gets a reminder; the rest does not.
  4. Keep errands apart. Groceries and pickups go in a second list so they do not crowd out real work.
  5. Close the day. Tick off what is done, move one leftover to tomorrow, delete what no longer matters.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mochi free?

Yes. The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set. Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.

Can I use Mochi as a checklist and a to do list?

Yes. The listing describes a fast checklist for tasks and errands, plus a daily planner, agenda and schedule, habit and routine tracking, notes and shopping lists.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. The App Store listing says you can check tasks from Apple Watch.

How many tasks should be on a daily to do list?

Around four that must happen today, with a separate list for errands. Everything else waits in a longer list or a note until you pick it for a specific day.

Do I need a separate app if I have Reminders?

Not always. Keep Reminders if you open it every day. A separate app helps when you want the list on your Home Screen in a widget and want ticking things off to feel more rewarding.
