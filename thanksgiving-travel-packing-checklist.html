Thanksgiving Travel Packing Checklist 2026: Pack Once, Forget Nothing

Short answer: pack for Thanksgiving in four groups. Clothes for four or five days including one dressier outfit for dinner; a carry-on with chargers, medicines and documents; anything the kids need on the road; and a short “before we leave the house” list. Thanksgiving 2026 is Thursday, November 26, so most people travel on Tuesday or Wednesday and come home over the weekend. Below is the full checklist with boxes you can copy, a four-item version that fits on one small list, and how to keep it in Mochi: Cute Checklist on your iPhone so you are not packing from memory at midnight.

The week before you travel

  • ☐ Confirm flights, train or driving plans and the time you need to leave
  • ☐ Check in online when it opens and save boarding passes to your phone
  • ☐ Ask the host what to bring: a dish, drinks, extra chairs or nothing at all
  • ☐ Plan outfits: travel clothes, a dinner outfit, something warm for cold evenings
  • ☐ Refill prescriptions so you do not run out while away
  • ☐ Arrange pet care, mail and package pickup
  • ☐ If driving, check tires, wipers and a phone mount before the road trip

Suitcase checklist

  • ☐ Outfits for each day, plus one dressier outfit for Thanksgiving dinner
  • ☐ Pajamas, underwear and socks for every night, plus one spare
  • ☐ A warm layer, a jacket and comfortable shoes for walks after dinner
  • ☐ Toiletries and any hair or skin products you use daily
  • ☐ A host gift (a candle, a plant, a dessert you picked up on the way)
  • ☐ Containers or bags if you plan to bring leftovers home
  • ☐ Laundry bag for worn clothes

Carry-on and travel day

If you are flying, the TSA page listed in the sources allows a quart-sized bag of liquids, aerosols, gels, creams and pastes in your carry-on, in travel-sized containers of 3.4 ounces (100 milliliters) or less per item. Homemade dishes that are liquid or spreadable count too, so check the TSA rules before packing pie fillings, sauces or gravy in a carry-on.

  • ☐ ID and boarding passes (paper backup if you like)
  • ☐ Phone charger, power bank and cables
  • ☐ Medicines in your carry-on, not in a checked bag
  • ☐ Quart-sized bag for travel-size liquids
  • ☐ Snacks, a refillable water bottle (empty at security) and headphones
  • ☐ House keys and car keys

Traveling with kids

  • ☐ A small bag of activities for the car or plane: books, crayons, a tablet with downloaded shows
  • ☐ Snacks and wipes within reach
  • ☐ A change of clothes in the carry-on
  • ☐ Favorite blanket or toy for sleeping somewhere new
  • ☐ Car seat or booster if you are renting a car or riding with family

Before you leave the house

  • ☐ Take out the trash and empty the fridge of anything that will spoil
  • ☐ Unplug small appliances and check the stove is off
  • ☐ Set the thermostat and lock windows and doors
  • ☐ Water plants and leave a light on a timer if you have one

This last list is the one people most often skip in the rush to the car, which is why it helps to keep it somewhere you will see it on the way out.

The short version: two lists of four

List “Pack for Thanksgiving”

  1. Dinner outfit and warm layer
  2. Chargers and power bank
  3. Medicines in the carry-on
  4. Host gift

List “Before we leave”

  1. Trash out, fridge cleared
  2. Stove off, appliances unplugged
  3. Thermostat set, doors locked
  4. Plants watered

Frequently asked questions

When is Thanksgiving 2026?

Thursday, November 26, 2026. Many people travel on Tuesday or Wednesday before it and return over the weekend.

Can I bring pie or gravy in my carry-on?

Liquids, gels, creams and pastes in a carry-on must follow the TSA rule: a quart-sized bag with travel-sized containers of 3.4 ounces (100 milliliters) or less per item. Check the TSA page for how a specific food is treated before you pack it.

What do people forget to pack for Thanksgiving?

Chargers, medicines, a dressier outfit for dinner, a host gift and the “before we leave the house” steps such as clearing the fridge and setting the thermostat.
