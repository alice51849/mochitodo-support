Daily checklist app: split your day into three short lists

One long to-do list for the whole day is easy to ignore. A daily checklist works better as a few short lists you can actually finish: one for the morning, one for the workday or school day, one for the evening. Here is how to set that up in Mochi on iPhone, with lists you can copy. Feature details are taken from Mochi’s App Store listing.

Why three short lists beat one long one

  • You can finish them. Four items is a list you can clear before you leave the house.
  • Each list has a moment. The morning list is for the morning; you are not staring at tonight’s jobs at 7 am.
  • It stays in sight. With a Home Screen widget or Apple Watch, you check the list without hunting for it.

Three daily checklists to copy

Morning

  • ☐ 💧 Glass of water
  • ☐ 🧴 Sunscreen on
  • ☐ 🎒 Bag, keys, Opal or myki card
  • ☐ 📅 Check today’s calendar

Workday or school day

  • ☐ ✅ The one task that matters most today
  • ☐ 📧 Reply to the email you have been avoiding
  • ☐ 🥪 Proper lunch break
  • ☐ 📝 Write tomorrow’s first task

Evening

  • ☐ 🍽️ Dishes done
  • ☐ 🧺 Washing on or off the line
  • ☐ 🗑️ Bins out on bin night
  • ☐ 📵 Phone away by 10

Set it up on iPhone

  1. Install Mochi and create a list called Morning and one called Evening (the free version covers 2 lists).
  2. Add up to four items to each and give every item an emoji you recognise at a glance.
  3. Set a reminder for anything with a time, such as bin night.
  4. Add the Mochi widget: touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Mochi. (The App Store listing does not say which plan the widget needs, so check in the app.)
  5. Tick items off with a tap on the little circle as you go.

Want the workday list as well, or more than four items? Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks.

Frequently asked questions

What is a daily checklist app?

An app for the short list of things you want done each day, kept somewhere you will see it. Mochi’s App Store listing describes a simple checklist for tasks and errands, a daily planner, agenda and schedule, and habit and routine tracking.

Can I see my daily checklist without opening the app?

Yes. Mochi has a Home Screen widget and an Apple Watch app.

Will it remind me?

Yes. Mochi includes reminders that help you stay on track.

How many lists can I make for free?

The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set. Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.
