Thanksgiving Dinner Checklist 2026: A Countdown You Can Copy

Short answer: work backward from the meal. Thanksgiving 2026 is Thursday, November 26. One week before, finalize the menu and shopping and start thawing a frozen turkey in time. The day before, prep sides, desserts and the table. On the day, follow a simple oven schedule and put leftovers away promptly. Below is the full countdown with checkboxes you can copy, a short “top 4” version for each stage, and how to keep it in Mochi: Cute Checklist on your iPhone.

Two weeks before (around November 12)

  • ☐ Confirm the guest count and any dietary needs
  • ☐ Plan the menu: turkey (or main dish), 3–5 sides, gravy, rolls, dessert, drinks
  • ☐ Decide what guests are bringing and write it down
  • ☐ Order a fresh turkey, or buy a frozen one early
  • ☐ Check you have a roasting pan, a food thermometer, serving dishes and enough chairs
  • ☐ Write the full shopping list, split into “keeps” and “buy fresh”

One week before (around November 19)

The thaw is the step people most often start too late. A frozen turkey thawed in the refrigerator needs about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds, according to the Minnesota Department of Health turkey fact sheet listed in the sources below. A 16–20 pound bird needs about 4 to 5 days, so for a Thursday dinner it usually goes into the refrigerator the Friday to Sunday before.

  • ☐ Buy everything on the “keeps” list (canned goods, baking supplies, drinks, foil)
  • ☐ Work out the thaw start day for your turkey's weight and set a reminder
  • ☐ Clear space in the refrigerator for the turkey and make-ahead dishes
  • ☐ Make and freeze anything that freezes well, like pie dough or rolls
  • ☐ Plan the oven schedule: what goes in when, and at what temperature
  • ☐ Send guests a reminder with the time and what they are bringing

The day before (Wednesday, November 25)

  • ☐ Buy the “buy fresh” items (herbs, salad greens, cream)
  • ☐ Check the turkey is thawing on schedule
  • ☐ Bake pies and other desserts
  • ☐ Chop vegetables and prepare sides that reheat well
  • ☐ Set the table and lay out serving dishes with a note of what goes in each
  • ☐ Chill drinks and make ice
  • ☐ Run the dishwasher and empty it so it is ready for the day

Thanksgiving Day (Thursday, November 26)

  • ☐ Take the turkey out of the refrigerator and start the oven schedule
  • ☐ Check doneness with a food thermometer instead of relying only on a pop-up timer
  • ☐ Let the turkey rest before carving while sides finish in the oven
  • ☐ Make the gravy and warm the rolls
  • ☐ Set out drinks and appetizers as guests arrive
  • ☐ Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours of serving
  • ☐ Pack take-home containers for guests

The 2-hour leftovers rule also comes from the Minnesota Department of Health turkey fact sheet. For cooking temperatures, follow the current guidance on your food thermometer and from food-safety authorities.

The short version: a &ldquo;top 4&rdquo; for each stage

One week before

  1. Menu and guest count final
  2. Shopping list done
  3. Turkey thaw start day set
  4. Refrigerator space cleared

The day before

  1. Desserts baked
  2. Sides prepped
  3. Table set
  4. Turkey thaw checked

Thanksgiving Day

  1. Turkey in the oven on time
  2. Sides and gravy
  3. Doneness checked with a thermometer
  4. Leftovers in the fridge within 2 hours

Frequently asked questions

When is Thanksgiving in 2026?

Thanksgiving in the United States is on Thursday, November 26, 2026 (the fourth Thursday of November).

When should I start thawing a frozen turkey?

Allow about 24 hours in the refrigerator for every 4 to 5 pounds, according to the Minnesota Department of Health turkey fact sheet. For a 16–20 pound turkey that is about 4 to 5 days, so start the Friday to Sunday before Thanksgiving.

What can I make the day before Thanksgiving?

Pies and other desserts, chopped vegetables, sides that reheat well, and the table setting. That leaves the oven and your attention for the turkey on the day.
