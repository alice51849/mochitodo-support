Mochi widgets: your checklist on the Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch

Mochi's checklist widget lets you see and tick off tasks without opening the app. This guide shows how to add each kind. Everything here matches what the app supports today: iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later.

Home Screen widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen until the icons wiggle.
  2. Tap Edit (or the + button) in the top corner, then choose Add Widget.
  3. Search for Mochi and pick the Mochi checklist widget.
  4. Swipe to the size you want — small, medium or large (iPad also has extra large) — and tap Add Widget, then Done.

Larger sizes show more tasks at once. The widget uses the same Mochi theme you picked in the app.

Tick tasks off from the widget

On iOS 17 and later you can tap the circle next to a task right on the widget — no need to open Mochi. When a list is longer than the widget can show, use the small arrow buttons to flip through the next tasks.

Lock Screen widget

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, then tap Customize and choose Lock Screen.
  2. Tap the area under the time to open the widget picker.
  3. Search for Mochi and choose a shape: rectangular shows your checklist, inline shows a single line with your next unfinished task (or an all-done message).
  4. Tap Done.

Apple Watch complication

Mochi includes an Apple Watch app. To show your progress on a watch face:

  1. On the watch, touch and hold the watch face, then tap Edit.
  2. Swipe to Complications, tap a slot and choose Mochi.

The circular style shows how many tasks are left inside a progress ring and the corner style shows the number with a curved progress bar; the rectangular style shows the list name, your next task and a progress bar; the inline style shows the number left.

Troubleshooting

I can't find Mochi in the widget list

Update Mochi to the latest version from the App Store, open it once, then try again. Widgets need iOS 17 or later.

The widget shows old tasks

Open Mochi once so it can refresh, or remove the widget and add it again. If it still looks wrong, restart your device.

The widget shows sample tasks, or tapping a task does nothing

Open Mochi once so it can load your list, then remove the widget and add it again.
