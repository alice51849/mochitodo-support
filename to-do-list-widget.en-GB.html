To-do list widget for iPhone: today’s tasks on your Home Screen

The easiest to-do list to keep up with is the one you see every time you unlock your phone. A Home Screen widget does exactly that: no app to open, no list to hunt for. Here is what to put on a to-do list widget, two lists you can copy, and how to set one up with Mochi on iPhone. Feature details are taken from Mochi’s App Store listing.

Why put your to-do list in a widget

  • You see it without trying. The list sits on the Home Screen, so you glance at it dozens of times a day.
  • It keeps the list honest. A widget only shows a handful of items, which nudges you to keep today’s list short.
  • It travels with you. Mochi also keeps your list on Apple Watch, so you can check it on the go.

What to put on a to-do list widget

Treat the widget as a window onto today, not your whole life. Three or four items is plenty: the one job that matters most, one errand, one thing for home and one small win. Everything else can wait in a second list inside the app.

Today (this one goes on the widget)

  • ☐ 📮 Post the parcel before the post office shuts
  • ☐ 🛒 Pick up milk and bread
  • ☐ 📞 Ring the GP surgery about that appointment

Later this week

  • ☐ 🧾 Pay the council tax reminder
  • ☐ 🎂 Card for Saturday’s birthday
  • ☐ 🚆 Check train times for the weekend
  • ☐ 🧺 Wash the bedding

Set it up on iPhone

  1. Install Mochi and create a list called Today, plus a second list for later in the week (the free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each).
  2. Give each task an emoji you recognise at a glance, so the widget reads in a second.
  3. Add the Mochi widget: touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Mochi. (The App Store listing does not say which plan the widget needs, so check in the app.)
  4. Set a reminder for anything with a time attached.
  5. Tap the little circle when a task is done.

Need more lists or more than four tasks? Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.
