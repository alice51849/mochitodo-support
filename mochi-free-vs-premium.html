Mochi free vs Premium: is the free version enough, and when is Premium worth it?

Short answer: if you want one cute list for today and one for groceries or errands, Mochi's free version already covers it. If you want separate lists for school, work, home, habits and a trip, each with more than four tasks, you will hit the free limits quickly, and that is what Premium is for. Below: what is in each version according to the US App Store listing, who should upgrade, and a one-week test so you can decide without guessing.

What Mochi is

From the App Store description: Mochi is a cute, cozy to-do list where every task can get its own little emoji. You tap the circle when you’re done, and you plan school, work, errands, groceries, habits and daily routines in one place.

  • Simple checklist for tasks and errands
  • Reminders that keep you on track
  • Daily planner, agenda and schedule
  • Habit and routine tracking
  • Notes, grocery lists, widgets and themes, with your list on a Home Screen widget and Apple Watch
  • Optimized for iPhone Duo: the app fills the entire inner display and adjusts its layout whether the device is folded or open

What the free version includes

The US App Store listing says the free version of Mochi includes:

  • 2 lists, with up to 4 tasks in each;
  • 5 themes out of 101;
  • 1 emoji set out of 11.

The way you use the app is the same in both versions: write down what you need to do, give it an emoji, tap the circle when it’s done and set a reminder so you don’t forget. Two lists of four go further than they sound if you choose them well: a Today list with the four things that really have to happen, and a Groceries or Don’t forget list.

What Premium adds

Premium is an in-app purchase. According to the listing, it unlocks:

  • unlimited lists and tasks: a list for each class, one for work, one for home, one for a trip, each with as many tasks as you need;
  • all 101 themes (the free version has 5);
  • all 11 emoji sets (the free version has 1).

The Home Screen widget and Apple Watch are described as part of Mochi; the listing does not name them among the things Premium unlocks. This page does not quote a price: see the App Store page for the current price.

How to decide in one week

  1. Install Mochi and stay on the free version for seven days.
  2. Make the two lists that matter most right now, for example Today and Groceries.
  3. Every time you want a third list or a fifth task and can’t add it, write down the date and what it was.
  4. At the end of the week, count. Once or twice means the free version is doing its job. Almost every day means the limit is really in your way, and Premium will get used.
  5. Also check whether you opened the app every day. If you didn’t, the limit isn’t the problem, and no purchase will fix that.

The test keeps you from buying on day one, when everything is new, and from giving up on the app because the free version felt too small before you set it up. For step-by-step setup, see the Mochi checklist guide and the widget guide.

Want to do this on your iPhone? Install Mochi, use the free version for a week and see whether 2 lists with 4 tasks each cover your day. If you run out of room, Premium unlocks unlimited lists and tasks inside the app.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mochi free?

Yes, Mochi is a free download. The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji set. Premium is an in-app purchase for people who need unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.

What does Mochi Premium unlock?

Unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets. The checklist, emoji on each task, the circle you tap and reminders work the same way in both versions.

How much does Mochi Premium cost?

This page does not quote a price. Premium is an in-app purchase; see the App Store page for the current price.

What happens when I reach the free limit?

The free version holds 2 lists with up to 4 tasks in each. A third list or a fifth task in the same list is where Premium comes in, since it unlocks unlimited lists and tasks.

Do the Home Screen widget and Apple Watch need Premium?

The App Store listing describes the Home Screen widget and Apple Watch as part of Mochi and does not list them among the things Premium unlocks.

Does Mochi have ads?

The privacy section of Mochi’s App Store page in the United States says “Data Not Collected”, and the Google Play listing does not show a “Contains ads” label (both checked on November 15, 2026).
